All GE flips

How it plans. You buy at the latest instasell price + 1 and sell at the latest instabuy price − 1. HC worlds have no GE tax, so margin is simply sell price minus buy price. The hourly fill rate is your share of the smaller side of traded volume, capped at a quarter of the 4-hour buy limit. Volume comes from the last 3 hours of 5-minute data where we have it, otherwise from the latest 5-minute window (marked 5m est). Cash goes to the best return per gp first. With Spread cash across slots on, each slot gets at most its share of your stack, so more slots get used, and any leftover tops up the best slots. HC worlds are new and thin, so check each margin with a quick buy/sell before you commit a full stack.
